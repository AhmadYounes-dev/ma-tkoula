About the game

ما تقولها (Ma T'oulha) is a word-guessing party game. On your turn, you explain a word to the others without saying any of its 5 rejected words, before the time runs out. Every word they guess wins a point, and saying a rejected word costs one. Play in two teams, or every player for themselves, with words in Arabic and English and packs for many dialects.

لعبة جماعية لتخمين الكلمات. في دورك، اشرح الكلمة للآخرين من دون أن تقول أيًّا من كلماتها المرفوضة الخمس قبل أن ينتهي الوقت. كل كلمة يخمّنونها تكسبكم نقطة، وكل كلمة مرفوضة تقولها تخسركم نقطة. العبوا فريقين أو كل لاعب لنفسه، على هاتف واحد تتناقلونه بينكم، أو يلعب كلٌّ على هاتفه عبر الإنترنت، بالواي فاي أو ببيانات الهاتف، أينما كنتم.

Two ways to play

On one device · على جهاز واحد

Up to 16 players share one phone and pass it on when a turn ends. This mode works fully offline.

Over Wi-Fi · عبر الواي فاي

Up to 10 players, each on their own phone, over Wi-Fi or mobile data, from anywhere. One player creates a room and shares its 8-digit code. The others tap Join Game (إنضم للعبة) and enter the code. Phones don't need to be on the same network or in the same place.

In an online room, the player who opened it runs it: they start the game and move everyone on to each new turn. If they leave, the player who has been in the room longest takes over.

A game between friends. Online rooms are private. Only the people you give the room code to can join.

How to play

  1. Choose how to play: on one device, or over Wi-Fi with each player on their own phone.
  2. Pick a save file (or make a new one) and add the players: two teams, or every player for themselves.
  3. On your turn, a main word appears with its 5 rejected words. Explain the main word without saying any of them, and without signs or sounds.
  4. Guessed it? Tap Correct for a point and get the next word. Stuck? Skip it, if skips are allowed in the settings.
  5. Use your abilities to help your team: Freeze Time (stops the timer for 7 seconds), Allow Word (one rejected word becomes allowed) and Use Signs (sounds and gestures). Opponent effects make the other side's turn harder.
  6. When the time runs out, the round ends and the turn passes on. The History button shows every round played so far.

Full rules, settings and examples are in How to play (كيفية اللعب) on the game's home screen.

Save files

Each mode has its own save files (up to 10). A save file remembers which words have already come up, so they don't repeat, even across games, until all the words in your packs have been used or you reset them. You choose a save file before each game. In an online room, the game uses the save file of the player who opened the room.

You can copy a save file to the other mode, or send it to a friend: your friend taps Receive and gives you the 6-digit code that appears, and you enter it after tapping Send.

My Words

In My Words (كلماتي) on the home screen, you can write up to 500 cards of your own, each a main word with 5 rejected words, sort them into folders, play them on one device or online, and send them to friends with a 6-digit code, the same way as a save file. Anyone can write words. Playing them is unlocked with the Full Bundle, or by owning all 22 packs.

Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

One-device mode works fully offline. Playing on separate phones (Over Wi-Fi) needs an internet connection on every phone. Wi-Fi and mobile data both work, and you don't need to be on the same network. You also need internet to send or receive a save file or words, to load ads, and to buy or restore word packs.

My friends can't join my room.

  • Check that they entered all 8 digits of the room code.
  • Make sure every phone is connected to the internet.
  • Make sure the room is still open. A room closes automatically about 5 minutes after everyone has left it. If it closed, open a new one.
  • Make sure everyone has the latest version of the game. The game asks you to update when a newer version is needed.

Someone lost their connection during a game.

The game keeps going for the others while their phone reconnects on its own. If they closed the app, they join the same room code again from the same phone and get their seat back with their score.

How do I choose a dialect?

In the game settings, on the Words tab, pick the packs you want to play: Arabic and English words, and dialect packs such as Lebanese, Egyptian, Gulf, Palestinian / Syrian, Iraqi, Sudanese, Yemeni and Maghrebi.

Will the same words come up again?

Not until you've been through all of them. The save file you play on remembers every word that has come up, even across games.

Does everyone in an online room need to buy a pack?

No. A pack owned by a player in the room can be played by the whole room while that player is there.

I bought a pack but it's still locked.

Make sure you are connected to the internet, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases (استعادة المشتريات). This checks your purchase with the App Store or Google Play again and unlocks it. Do the same after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Are our scores saved?

Team names, players and scores are kept in your save file on your phone, so you can carry on a game later. Online rooms are deleted from our server automatically about 5 minutes after everyone has left.

I found a bug, or I have an idea.

Email us with what happened, which mode you were playing (one device or Over Wi-Fi), and your phone model. A screenshot helps a lot.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information ما تقولها ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account, login or sign-up of any kind. We do not ask for your email address, phone number, contacts or precise location, and the App does not access your photos, camera, microphone or files.

Player Names and Game Data

To play, you type a name for each player. Names are used only to show whose turn it is and to keep score:

  • In one-device mode, names, teams and scores stay on your phone, in your save file. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • In Over Wi-Fi mode, the App sends the following to our game server so the room can run: the name you enter, the room code, and your in-game actions (such as correct and skipped words, rejected words said, abilities used and scores). For the player who opens the room, it also sends their selected save file's name, teams, scores, settings and progress (which words have already come up). The server shares names, actions and scores with the other players in the same room, as the game requires.
  • Room data is kept on the server only while the room is in use, and is deleted automatically about 5 minutes after everyone has left the room.
  • Other players in your room can see the name you enter. You are free to use a nickname.

Save Files and My Words

Save files and your own words (My Words) are stored on your device. A save file contains the name you gave it, the player and team names, scores, settings and which words have already come up. Copying a save file to the other mode happens entirely on your device.

When you send a save file or words to a friend, they are uploaded to our server and held only until your friend's phone receives them. They are deleted automatically, at the latest 10 minutes after your friend's code was created.

Online, if you switch your My Words on in a room, the App sends those words to the room so they can be played there. They are shown only to the players in that room, and deleted with the room. We do not read, sell or share your words, and do not use them for any other purpose.

Our Game Server

Online rooms and file transfers run on our own server, hosted by Cloudflare. All connections to it are encrypted. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address to deliver the connection, and keeps short technical logs for a few days, which we use only to keep the service working and fix problems. The server also counts how many players are connected at the moment, without names, so we can see how busy the game is. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not sell or share it.

Google Firebase

The App uses Google Firebase for the following:

  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics tells us how the App is used, for example how many people play, which screens they open, how long a session lasts and when a purchase is made. It uses a random identifier created by the App (and, where your device allows it, its advertising identifier), together with basic information such as your device model, operating system, App version and general region (worked out from your IP address). We use this only to understand and improve the game, not to identify you.
  • Update check: when the App starts, it asks Firebase Remote Config whether a newer version of the App is required.
  • Anonymous sign-in and purchase check: the App signs in to Firebase anonymously, which gives it a random user ID with no name, email or other details attached. This ID is used for the purchase check described under In-App Purchases below.

Firebase is operated by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally on your own device:

  • Your save files and My Words, and which save file is selected in each mode.
  • The name you used for online play, so you don't have to type it again.
  • Which word packs you own, so they stay unlocked.
  • Your settings and your ad consent choices, where they apply.

Save files and words only leave your device as described above. All of this information is removed if you uninstall the App.

Advertising

The App shows ads served through Google AdMob, with Unity Ads as an additional ad partner: banner ads, full-screen ads between rounds, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for a bonus (20 extra seconds or double points) or to try 5 free words from a pack. Buying a word pack removes all ads.

To serve and measure ads, these ad networks may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, basic device information, and how you interact with ads. We do not control this data collection directly. It is handled by Google and Unity under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · Unity Privacy Policy. You can also read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from Privacy Settings in the App's settings. On iPhone, if the App asks for permission to track your activity, you can decline. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.

You can limit ad personalization at any time from your device's settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising on iOS).

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional word packs. The free version of the game is fully playable without buying anything.

Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). To make sure a purchase is genuine, the App sends the purchase receipt issued by the store to our purchase-check service (Google Cloud Functions, through Firebase), which asks Google Play or Apple to confirm it. When a purchase is confirmed, we keep a record of it (the product, the store's order or transaction number and the date), linked only to the App's anonymous Firebase user ID, so the purchase can be restored. The receipt and this record contain no payment details.
